# NiMH battery pack

Zeee NiMH 7.2 V 3600 mAh pack with an EC5 plug. Status: as built.

## Purpose

Main power for the motors through the kill switch and the L298N.

## Fit and position

The EC5 lead hangs between the rear motors. Where the pack body rests is not recorded.

# L298N motor driver

L298N dual H-bridge driver module (generic clone, size unverified). Status: as built.

## Purpose

Drives the four TT gear motors from the NiMH pack, under control of the Pi 4.

## Fit and position

Top deck, rear left, next to the kill switch. Raised on brass standoffs above the plate.

## Mounting points

- Brass standoffs from the board's mounting holes to the top deck (count, thread and height not measured).

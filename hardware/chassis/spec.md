# Chassis

Black brushed aluminium two-deck chassis with vertical side brackets. Status: as built.

## Purpose

Carries the motors, electronics and sensors. The top deck holds the electronics and the pan servo; the lower deck holds the motor bolts and the front bumper extension.

## Fit and position

Two plates, 56 mm apart. The top deck is a rounded rectangle with edge slots, a central rectangular cut-out and diagonal slots at the front corners. The lower deck is narrower. It has a slotted front bumper extension and two oval cut-outs per side that expose the motor bolts. The bumper extension is part of the lower plate and sits directly in front of the front tyres. An unused sensor bracket hangs below the front edge of the top deck.

## Mounting points

- The decks are joined by vertical metal side brackets.
- The side brackets also clamp the motors with bolts through the motor bodies (thread size is a photo reading only; see Open questions).
- The unused sensor bracket (two small holes, one large round hole) is fixed to the front edge of the top deck.

# Pan servo

SM-S2309S servo. Status: as built.

## Purpose

Pans the PING))) sensor left and right.

## Fit and position

Front centre of the top deck, hot-glued to the plate. The PING))) sensor is glued to its horn.

## Mounting points

- Hot glue to the top deck plate. Its mounting tabs are not used.

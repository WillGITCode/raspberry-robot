# Checklist of values to measure

One line per TBD row in the specs. Tick a line, then put the value in the spec and change its source to `measured`.

## chassis

- [ ] `top_deck_corner_radius` (mm): Outer corner radius. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `top_deck_edge_slot_size` (mm): Length and width of one edge slot. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `top_deck_edge_slot_positions` (mm): Offset of each edge slot from the plate edges. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `top_deck_cutout_length` (mm): Central rectangular cut-out, front to back. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `top_deck_cutout_width` (mm): Central rectangular cut-out, side to side. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `top_deck_cutout_offset` (mm): Cut-out position from the front edge. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `top_deck_diagonal_slot_size` (mm): Length and width of a front-corner diagonal slot. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `top_deck_diagonal_slot_angle` (deg): Angle of the front-corner diagonal slots. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `lower_deck_oval_cutout_length` (mm): One of two oval cut-outs per side. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `lower_deck_oval_cutout_width` (mm): One of two oval cut-outs per side. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `lower_deck_oval_cutout_positions` (mm): Offset of each oval from the front edge and the side edge. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `bumper_hole_diameter` (mm): The two round holes in the bumper extension. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `bumper_hole_positions` (mm): Position of each round hole. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `bumper_slot_size` (mm): One slot in the row on the bumper extension. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `bumper_slot_pitch` (mm): Centre to centre spacing of the slots. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `bumper_corner_angle` (deg): Angle of the cut front corners. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `side_bracket_height` (mm): Vertical extent of one side bracket. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `side_bracket_width` (mm): Width of one side bracket. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `side_bracket_thickness` (mm): Metal thickness of one side bracket. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `side_bracket_position` (mm): Position along the deck edge. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `motor_bolt_thread` (mm): Photo reading says M3; confirm. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `sensor_bracket_hole_diameter_small` (mm): Two small holes. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `sensor_bracket_hole_diameter_large` (mm): One large round hole. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `sensor_bracket_hole_positions` (mm): Positions of the three holes on the bracket. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).
- [ ] `sensor_bracket_size` (mm): Length, width and metal thickness of the bracket. Measure with a tape or ruler (the calliper display is faulty).

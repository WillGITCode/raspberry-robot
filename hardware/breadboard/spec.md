# Breadboard

Solderless breadboard, which one is TBD (full, half and mini are on hand). Status: planned.

## Purpose

Carries the robot's wiring: the 5 V and ground rails, the PING resistor, the servo and encoder signals, and spare rows for the GY-521 (see `hardware/wiring.md`).

## Fit and position

Top deck, in a printed holder with a hold-down so jumpers do not shake loose while driving. Exact position TBD.

## Mounting points

A printed holder with a hold-down, bolted through the existing deck slots with M3 screws (decisions 4 and 5). The holder is not designed yet.

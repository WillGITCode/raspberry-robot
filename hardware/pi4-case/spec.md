# Raspberry Pi 4 in its case

Raspberry Pi 4 Model B in a black finned case. Status: as built.

## Purpose

Main computer of the robot. Runs the control code.

## Fit and position

Top deck, rear right. The USB-C, 2 x micro-HDMI and audio side faces rearward; the USB and Ethernet end faces right. Nothing is plugged into the USB-C in the 2026-11-09 photos.

## Mounting points

- How the case is fixed to the top deck is not recorded (see Open questions).

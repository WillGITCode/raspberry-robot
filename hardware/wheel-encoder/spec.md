# Wheel encoder

10-slot encoder, generic. Status: as built.

## Purpose

Counts wheel rotation on the two front motors. Unwired at present.

## Fit and position

On the front two TT motors. The leads are twisted red/green/black with 3-pin Dupont plugs.

## Mounting points

Not yet recorded (see Open questions).

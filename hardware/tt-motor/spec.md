# TT motor

Yellow TT gear motor, generic. Status: as built.

## Purpose

Drives one wheel. Four are fitted, one per corner.

## Fit and position

Clamped between the side brackets at each corner of the lower deck. The bolt heads show through the oval cut-outs in the lower deck. The EC5 battery lead hangs between the two rear motors. The two front motors carry wheel encoders.

## Mounting points

Bolts (M3-size, photo reading) through the motor body, clamped by the vertical side brackets.

# Wheel

65 mm TT-motor wheel, generic. Status: as built.

## Purpose

Rolls the robot. Four are fitted, one on each TT motor shaft.

## Fit and position

On the output shaft of each TT motor, outside the lower deck. The front tyres sit directly behind the bumper extension.

## Mounting points

Press fit on the D-shaft of the TT motor.

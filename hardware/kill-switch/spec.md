# Kill switch

Mini toggle switch, ON-ON, 3 terminals. Status: as built.

## Purpose

Cuts or switches the NiMH pack lead to the electronics.

## Fit and position

Through the rear of the top deck, beside the L298N. It sits in the NiMH lead.

## Mounting points

- Nut through a hole in the top deck (hole size and nut type not measured).
